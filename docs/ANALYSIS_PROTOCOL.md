# Frozen definitive joint synthetic control — methodological protocol

- **Version status:** post-hoc design frozen after inspection of earlier storm outcomes. This is *not* preregistration. **Revised 14 November 2026** (synthetic-DiD pre-trend redesign, see the last section); the earlier 2022–23-fit version and its outputs are kept under `archive/2026-11-14_before_sdid_refit/`.
- **Source data:** fresh raw-source reconstruction in this repository. LAUS defaults to an explicitly identified third-party BLS-series distributor; QCEW/NOAA/FEMA/Census are official source downloads or verified local source records. SHA-256 manifest generated for raw inputs.
- **Estimand:** equal-county mean among 39 frozen FEMA-positive North Carolina counties versus geographically isolated untreated national counties. Exposure-based selection is observational.
- **Donors:** state exclusions frozen from geographic policy; all four outcomes complete for January 2022–December 2023. Neither 2024 holdout nor post-event observations select donors.
- **One, and only one, control weight vector:** optimize four 2022-centered outcomes together: resident employment 100×log, resident labor force 100×log, QCEW jobs 100×log, LAUS unemployment rate pp. Floors 0.25 for logs and 0.15 pp for rate, scales from 2022.
- **Regularization:** nonnegative simplex donor weights, ridge penalty toward uniform donor weights. Fixed lambda grid {.03,.1,.3,1,3,10}; train on 2022, score on 2023, 1%-near-best tie toward stronger regularization. The penalty protocol is unchanged; the **final weights are then fitted on the full pre-event window, January 2022–August 2024, with a free intercept per outcome** (each outcome block is demeaned over the fit window), i.e. synthetic-DiD unit weights that match pre-event movements rather than levels.
- **Validation:** there is no untouched holdout for the definitive weights, because 2024 is inside their fit window. Out-of-sample evidence comes from **backdated weights** (same objective, intercept and lambda, fitted on 2022–23 only) evaluated on January–August 2024; all four RMSEs, the ratio to training error, slopes and in-time placebo contrasts are reported even if poor; no retuning.
- **Main inference:** weighted TWFE event study; coefficients and window effects are expressed **relative to the pre-event mean (January 2022–August 2024, uniform time weights)**, not to August 2024 alone; September transition excluded; six matched outcomes through March 2026 with October 2025 LAUS missing (not zero) and three prespecified windows. Holm family comprises 18 hypotheses.
- **Pre-event diagnostics:** linear pre-trend contrast, equality of half-year bin means, number and size of individually significant monthly pre-event coefficients, joint test of January–July 2024 against August 2024, joint test of all 31 monthly coefficients. All are reported; none selects the model.
- **Exploratory:** LAUS through September 2026, local-linear Gaussian intensity curves and county-bootstrap intervals (each county measured against its own pre-event mean gap, plus a January–August 2024 placebo window measured against its 2022–23 mean gap, and linear exposure gradients per SD), annual Census net migration.
- **Spatial placebos:** refit all four-outcome weights for 39-county pseudo-treated donor groups, using frozen primary lambda and the same full-pre-event intercept fit; shocks are October–December 2024 minus the pre-event mean gap. Rankings are descriptive rather than randomized causal p-values. These are NOT the prior employment-only placebo ranks.
- **Limitations:** county-clustered SEs omit some correlated regional shocks and donor-weight estimation uncertainty; migration components do not identify whether original residents departed; different QCEW/LAUS populations.
- **Deprecated research specifications:** no historical employment-only synthetic weights, historical models, alternative single-outcome fits or outputs are included in this repository.

## Revision of 14 November 2026: why and what

**Diagnosis.** The earlier weights were fitted on 2022–23 and matched 2022-centered levels. Against them the treated counties' resident employment and labor force fell by about 0.6–0.9 log-% in June–August 2024, before the storm, and the event study took the last of those months (August 2024) as its only reference. Every pre-event coefficient was therefore shifted by the same amount and the post-event losses were understated. The same June–August 2024 drop appears in the 61 *untreated* North Carolina counties measured against the same synthetic control, so it is a state-wide LAUS movement that out-of-state donors chosen on 2022–23 did not reproduce, not something specific to the storm area. QCEW jobs do not show it.

**Change.** (1) Unit weights use every pre-event month and an intercept, as in synthetic DiD. (2) The reference is the pre-event mean instead of one month. (3) The Gaussian exposure curves use each county's pre-event mean gap as baseline and gain a pre-event placebo window. Lambda selection, donors, treated roster, outcomes, windows and the Holm family are unchanged.

**What this does and does not buy.** The pre-event gap is now flat on the surface: no drift, no visible summer-2024 gap, no individually significant monthly coefficient for the four fitted outcomes. That flatness is in-sample. The backdated weights still mispredict January–August 2024 for resident employment and labor force (out-of-sample RMSE 4–6 times training RMSE), and the strict joint tests still reject exact month-by-month equality, most clearly for the unemployment rate and the two ratio outcomes, where the remaining deviations are small (about 0.06 pp RMSE for the rate) but precisely estimated. A formal synthetic-DiD time-weight vector was checked outside the pipeline and moves the window effects by at most about 0.25 log-% (workplace jobs) and 0.05 or less for the other outcomes; uniform time weights are used for transparency.
